SOUL CLASH — FULL RULES REPORT
================================

GAME OVERVIEW
-------------
- Single PC, pass-and-play. The PC screen is a shared "game progress dashboard"
  showing state (hearts, shields, marks visible where applicable, charges, etc).
- A human moderator runs the dashboard and privately handles any hidden
  information (e.g. Akyros's Hidden Mark) and randomizers (coin flips, RPS).

MODES
-----
- 2-Player: each player picks 2 characters.
- 4-Player: each player picks 1 character.

TURN STRUCTURE
---------------
- A "round" = one player's full turn, taken in seat order
  (P1 -> P2 [-> P3 -> P4]).
- On their turn, a player acts with ALL of their non-KO'd characters. For each
  character, the player freely chooses which available action to use (normal
  ability or special, subject to that character's own gating rules described
  below).
- Targeting is always fully manual: the active player chooses any enemy
  character for each ability use, unless a card's own rules restrict this
  (e.g. Blade's streak tracking, Akyros's marks, Athena's curse target).

LIFE / SHIELD SYSTEM
---------------------
- Every character has 7 hearts (lives). Damage subtracts directly from
  hearts. 0 hearts = KO'd.
- Shields absorb incoming damage before hearts are touched, but shield
  behavior differs per character (see individual sections below) — some are
  permanent stacking pools, others decay after 1 round.
- A KO'd character is removed from play. A player is only eliminated once ALL
  of their characters are KO'd. Last player standing wins the match.
- Exception: Blade's Rebirth special (see below) can bring him back once.


===========================================================
1. CHRONOX — Time Controller
===========================================================

Chrono Guard (passive, always on)
- Chronox has 1 shield active from the very start of the match, before
  anyone has taken a turn.
- At the start of each of Chronox's own turns, his shield RESETS to exactly
  1 - it does not stack or accumulate turn to turn.
- Within a single round (before the next reset), the shield is a depleting
  pool consumed hit-by-hit as damage lands. If Chronox is hit multiple
  times in the same round (e.g. by two different enemy characters in a
  2v2), the 1 shield absorbs the first point of damage from the first hit
  and is then exhausted, so a second hit that round lands at full damage.

Cyclone Punch (normal action, every turn, any target)
- Flip a coin: Heads -> target loses 2 hearts. Tails -> target loses 1 heart.
  (Damage applies after shield absorption as normal.)

Time Freeze (special, 1x per match, targets one enemy)
- Initial use: the target skips their very next turn. No coin flip needed
  for this first skip.
- From Chronox's NEXT turn onward, EVERY subsequent turn of his triggers an
  automatic bonus coin flip — regardless of who he actually attacks that
  turn with Cyclone Punch (he is free to attack anyone, not just the frozen
  target):
    Heads -> freeze continues, frozen target skips their next turn again.
    Tails -> freeze ends permanently.
- Only the frozen character skips their own turn — not their whole player's
  turn (relevant when a player controls 2 characters).


===========================================================
2. THAROX — Titan Warrior
===========================================================

Four possible actions, player's free choice each turn (subject to gating):

Smash (normal, any target)
- Flat -1 heart. No conditions.

Titan Toss (normal, no damage/target)
- Grants Tharox a "Titan Smash charge." Only usable if Tharox does NOT
  already hold a charge.

Titan Smash (normal, follow-up, any target)
- Deals -3 hearts. Only usable while holding a charge (from Titan Toss OR
  from Glory Smash). Consumes the charge. This ends the combo.

Glory Smash (special, 1x per match, follow-up, any target)
- Deals -2 hearts to the target, plus Tharox gains +2 hearts (self-heal) and
  +2 shield for 1 round only (decays after, even if untouched — unlike
  Chronox's permanent stacking shield).
- Only usable while holding a charge. Consumes the current charge AND
  grants a brand-new Titan Smash charge in its place, allowing a further
  Titan Smash later.

MANDATORY CASH-IN: after Titan Toss (or after Glory Smash grants a new
charge), Tharox's very next actionable turn MUST be Titan Smash or Glory
Smash — he cannot use Smash instead, and cannot Toss again while holding a
charge. The only thing that delays this obligation is being frozen (e.g. by
Chronox's Time Freeze) and skipping a turn — the charge simply waits, and
the mandatory-use requirement resumes on his next actionable turn once
unfrozen. Taking damage from other players in between does not affect the
charge or the obligation.

Max chain: Toss -> Glory Smash -> Titan Smash (3 turns, only possible once
per match since Glory Smash is 1x). Shorter chain Toss -> Titan Smash can
recur any number of times across the match.


===========================================================
3. ZERATHYS — Storm Mage
===========================================================

Two normal actions, player's free choice each turn:

Charge Up (normal, no damage/target)
- +1 to Zerathys's charge counter (max 2, caps there). Banked charge
  persists indefinitely across turns until spent on Thunder Wrath — it does
  not decay on its own and survives skipped turns.

Thunder Wrath (normal, any target)
- Deals damage based on CURRENT charge level, then resets the charge to 0
  afterward:
    0 Charge -> -1 heart
    1 Charge -> -2 hearts
    2 Charges -> -3 hearts
- To repeatedly hit for -3, Zerathys must re-charge twice between each use
  (Charge, Charge, Wrath, Charge, Charge, Wrath...). Attacking every turn
  without charging just chips -1 heart each time.

Soul Swap (special, 1x per match, targets any enemy)
- Swaps CURRENT hearts totals between Zerathys and the target. (Smart play:
  swap when the target has MORE hearts than Zerathys, stealing their higher
  pool and dumping his lower total onto them.)
- Immediately follows with a free Thunder Wrath (does not cost a separate
  turn) against ANY target (not necessarily the same one just swapped
  with), using whatever charge is currently banked — that charge resets to
  0 afterward as normal.


===========================================================
4. AKYROS — Shadow Assassin
===========================================================

Hidden Mark (normal action, no damage)
- Secretly marks a target. Since this is a shared single-dashboard game,
  the player tells the moderator privately which enemy to mark; the
  dashboard does not reveal it.
- Multiple enemies can be marked at the same time. Marking a new target does
  NOT remove existing marks. Marks are PERMANENT — they never expire or
  auto-clear for the rest of the match.
- A mark is only revealed to other players once Fatal Slash is used against
  that marked target.
- The player may also re-mark an already-marked target. This has no extra
  mechanical effect — it's a legal "pass"/stalling move used purely for
  strategy (e.g. baiting opponents, avoiding revealing info yet).

Fatal Slash (normal action, every turn, any target)
- Marked target -> -2 hearts (this reveals that the target was marked).
- Unmarked target -> -1 heart.

Dodge (passive, always on)
- Per-attacker tracking: the very FIRST attack any given enemy character
  EVER lands on Akyros (across the whole match, not per-round) deals 0
  damage. Once that enemy's first hit has been negated, all of their
  subsequent attacks on Akyros land normally for the rest of the match.
  Each enemy character has their own independently tracked "first hit."

Shadow Execution (special, 1x per match)
- Requires at least one active mark to exist somewhere among the enemies.
  Player chooses any currently marked enemy as the target: -3 hearts,
  ignoring shields entirely.
- Does NOT consume/remove the mark — it remains on that target afterward
  (since marks never expire).


===========================================================
5. VELORYA — Moon Assassin
===========================================================

Turn 1 of the match
- Velorya's very first attack must be Lunar Strike, OR she may instead open
  directly with Lunar Eclipse (special). Moonstep is not available yet since
  there is no prior target to compare against.

Lunar Strike (normal action, any target)
- Flat -1 heart, ignoring shields.

Moonstep (normal action, any target, available from her 2nd attack onward)
- Compares ONLY to her immediately preceding attack's target (not full
  history):
    Different target than her last attack -> -2 hearts, ignoring shields.
    Same target as her last attack -> -1 heart, ignoring shields (same as
    Lunar Strike).
- Ping-ponging between two targets each turn nets -2 hearts every time,
  since the target always differs from the immediately prior one.

Lunar Eclipse (special, 1x per match, no target/self-buff)
- Velorya becomes untargetable by enemy attacks. She still takes her normal
  turns (Lunar Strike/Moonstep) while this is active.
- After each of her attacks during this state, flip a coin:
    Heads -> untargetable continues.
    Tails -> untargetable ends permanently (single use, like all specials).

Interaction with Athena: Velorya's untargetable status does NOT block
Athena's Curse Strike mirrored damage (see Athena section) — because that
damage isn't a targeted attack selection against Velorya, it's a side-effect
mirror of damage Athena herself takes.


===========================================================
6. BOINGO — Chaos Trickster
===========================================================

Chaos Gamble (normal action, every turn, any target)
- The moderator resolves a Rock-Paper-Scissors matchup between Boingo and
  the target off-dashboard (no separate dashboard input needed — moderator
  just applies the result):
    Boingo wins -> target -3 hearts (Giant Explosive Punch)
    Draw -> target -1 heart (Giant Punch)
    Boingo loses -> 0 hearts, no effect (Miss)

Jester Ball (special, 1x per match, thrown at one target)
- Does NOT resolve immediately. The ball sits with the current holder until
  THAT PLAYER'S OWN next turn, where handling the ball becomes their action
  for that turn. The holder must choose one of:
    Return to Boingo -> Boingo gains +4 hearts (self-heal).
    Pass to a different player -> that player becomes the new holder, same
      deal applies on their own next turn — BUT only one pass total is
      allowed for the whole Jester Ball sequence.
    Take it -> holder loses -4 hearts.
- Once the ball has been passed once, the second (final) holder no longer
  has the pass option — only Return (Boingo +4 hearts) or Take (-4 hearts to
  self) are available to them.


===========================================================
7. BLADE — Blood Hunter
===========================================================

Blood Hunt (normal action, every turn, any target)
- Tracks a consecutive-hit streak against whichever single target Blade has
  been attacking.
    1st hit on a given target: -1 heart.
    Each consecutive hit on the SAME target (2nd, 3rd, ...): damage
    increases by +1 heart per hit in the streak (-1, -2, -3, -4, -5, ...),
    with no cap.
- Switching to a DIFFERENT target fully resets the streak to -1 heart on the
  new target — no memory of the previous target's streak count is kept.
- If Blade is frozen/skips a turn (e.g. via Chronox's Time Freeze), the
  streak does NOT reset — it pauses and resumes from where it left off once
  he acts again against the same target.

Rebirth (special, 1x per match)
- Automatic (not player-chosen) the instant Blade would be KO'd, if the
  special hasn't been used yet. Revives him with 2 hearts. Does not cost a
  turn/action.
- One-time only — if he is KO'd again after Rebirth, he is out of the match
  for good.


===========================================================
8. ATHENA — Curse Guardian
===========================================================

Curse Strike (normal action, every turn, VISIBLE to all players — unlike
Akyros's Hidden Mark)
- Choose one player to curse. Only ONE curse is active at a time — choosing
  a new target moves the curse there and removes it from whoever was
  previously cursed.
- Choosing the same already-cursed target again is a valid strategic
  pass/no-op move.
- Curse mechanic: while a curse is active, whenever ATHENA takes damage from
  any attack (from anyone), the CURSED player also takes that same amount of
  damage as a mirrored copy. Athena still takes her own damage normally too
  — this is not a redirect away from her; both she and the cursed player
  take the hit.
- The mirrored damage to the cursed player still passes through THEIR OWN
  shield normally (can be reduced/absorbed) — it does NOT ignore shields
  (unlike Akyros's Shadow Execution, which does ignore shields).
- Confirmed interaction: this mirrored damage is NOT blocked by Velorya's
  Lunar Eclipse untargetable status, because it isn't a targeted attack
  selection against the cursed player — it's a side-effect mirror triggered
  by Athena being hit.

Divine Restore (special, 1x per match)
- Athena gains +3 hearts (self-heal) and +2 shield for 1 round only (decays
  after, same pattern as Tharox's Glory Smash shield).

Note: Athena has no other direct-damage normal ability. Curse Strike (the
curse placement itself) is her full normal action each turn — she has no
separate attack move.
